CoreCV is moving beyond a traditional waitlist.
The goal of the founding cohort is to test the core thesis behind CoreCV:
Can a professional build a credible, evidence-backed record of what they can 
actually do, and can companies use that record to discover and evaluate 
talent without relying entirely on the traditional CV/application process?
The founding program therefore needs both sides of the market:
Professionals → build credible records
Hiring partners → discover and evaluate those records
The campaign should build both sides simultaneously, with greater initial emphasis on 
securing hiring partners.
Selected early-career and growing tech professionals join a structured cohort.
Over a defined period, participants build their CoreCV professional record:
CoreCV helps participants turn scattered evidence into a coherent professional record.
